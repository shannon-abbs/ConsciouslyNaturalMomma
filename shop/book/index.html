Why I Write to Empower Like-Minded Mothers on Their Conscious, Natural Parenting Journey

My Story:
Before I became pregnant with my first daughter, W, I made a promise to myself: If I ever had a child, I would commit to becoming the healthiest version of myself. At the time, I thought this meant focusing solely on food and exercise. But as my pregnancy journey unfolded, I realized that becoming the best version of myself meant changing my entire lifestyle—for both me and my baby.

At that point, I hadn’t fully embraced a natural mindset yet. But after stressing for 2.4 years to conceive, I knew I wanted to pursue a natural path to pregnancy and motherhood. However, it wasn’t easy. I faced doubts from family and doctors who told me what I “couldn’t” do. But deep down, I knew that other women around the world were walking this path with success, and I had to find the courage to follow it myself.

So, I dove into countless books, blogs, and online communities—searching for guidance. The more I explored, though, the more fragmented the information felt. It was like piecing together a puzzle without a clear picture.

What I needed was one complete resource to guide me. That’s why I decided to write it. This book is the culmination of everything I’ve learned—from conception through the “terrific twos”—created for fellow mindful, conscious, and naturally-minded mothers who want to embrace this journey with confidence, ease, and purpose.

I’ve designed a holistic, consciously natural motherhood lifestyle that flows seamlessly, making day-to-day parenting easier. With this guide, my hope is to empower you with a clear path forward—one that helps you feel confident, supported, and deeply connected as you navigate your own unique journey through motherhood.
